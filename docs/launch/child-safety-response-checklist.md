# Hellowhen child-safety report operational checklist

Use this internal checklist for reports involving suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation. Keep report contents, personal data, moderation notes, and evidence within the authorized report, support, and moderation systems.

- [ ] Confirm `support@hellowhen.com` is monitored and the designated Child Safety Point of Contact in Play Console is current, reachable, and able to speak to Hellowhen’s enforcement and review procedures.
- [ ] Receive the child-safety report through the in-app report queue, support ticket, or `support@hellowhen.com`, and link it to the relevant internal record.
- [ ] Restrict access to potentially harmful content when appropriate to reduce further exposure while the report is reviewed.
- [ ] Escalate the report to an authorized reviewer for child-safety assessment.
- [ ] Remove prohibited content and restrict or suspend the responsible account when the violation is confirmed and those actions are appropriate.
- [ ] Preserve only information legitimately required for safety review, moderation records, legal compliance, or required reporting, with access limited to authorized personnel.
- [ ] Report confirmed child sexual abuse material to the relevant competent authority where required by applicable law.
- [ ] Document the review outcome, content/account action, required external report, and the internal records supporting the decision.

Do not copy prohibited material into general documentation, tickets, chat, or release notes. Do not promise a response deadline or external reporting obligation beyond what applicable law requires.
